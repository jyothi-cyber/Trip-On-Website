Tour Overview

Experience the breathtaking beauty of Phi Phi Islands on this full-day adventure from Phuket. Visit the iconic Maya Bay, snorkel in crystal-clear waters, and enjoy a delicious Thai lunch with stunning sea views.

  • Visit the famous Maya Bay and Pileh Lagoon
  • Snorkel at Shark Point and discover colorful marine life
  • Enjoy a buffet lunch at a beachfront restaurant
  • Explore Monkey Beach and Viking Cave
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off included
  • Professional English-speaking guide
