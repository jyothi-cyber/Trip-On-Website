Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Bali - Seminyak

Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport. Our representative will meet you and transfer you to your hotel in Seminyak. Check-in and relax. Evening free for leisure. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 2: Tanah Lot Temple & Seminyak Beach

After breakfast, visit the iconic Tanah Lot Temple, one of Bali's most photographed landmarks. Explore the temple perched on a rock formation in the sea. Afternoon free at Seminyak Beach. Witness a spectacular sunset. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 3: Full Day Ubud Tour

After breakfast, drive to Ubud. Visit Tegalalang Rice Terrace, Ubud Monkey Forest, Ubud Palace, and Ubud Art Market. Optional visit to a coffee plantation. Return to hotel. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 4: Water Temple & Uluwatu Temple

Visit Tirta Empul Water Temple in the morning. Afternoon drive to Uluwatu Temple, perched on a cliff overlooking the Indian Ocean. Watch the famous Kecak Fire Dance at sunset. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 5: Nusa Penida Island Day Trip

After breakfast, take a speedboat to Nusa Penida. Visit Kelingking Beach (T-Rex), Angel's Billabong, Broken Beach, and Crystal Bay. Snorkeling opportunity (optional). Return to hotel in the evening. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 6: Spa & Leisure

After breakfast, enjoy a relaxing traditional Balinese spa treatment (60 minutes). Rest of the day at leisure for shopping or beach activities. Overnight in Seminyak.

Day 7: Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel. Transfer to Ngurah Rai International Airport for your onward journey with wonderful memories of Bali.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a visa for Bali?
A: Indian citizens get 30-day visa on arrival. Passport must be valid for 6 months.

Q: What is the best time to visit Bali?
A: April to October is the dry season and best time to visit. June to September is peak tourist season.

Q: Is this package customizable?

Q: What type of hotels are included?

Q: Are meals included?
