Package Overview

Discover the raw, untouched drama of Nusa Penida — Bali's most spectacular offshore island. This 5-day package takes you through sheer limestone cliffs, crystal-clear bays, and pristine white beaches that feel worlds away from the mainland. The iconic Kelingking Beach T-Rex cliffs and the manta ray snorkelling at Crystal Bay are experiences that define bucket-list travel.

Nusa Penida remains bracingly wild and authentic compared to mainland Bali. Roads are rough, the scenery is jaw-dropping, and the rewards for those who make the journey are extraordinary. Expert guides ensure you see the best of the island safely and comfortably.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival & Fast Boat to Nusa Penida

Arrive at Bali airport. Transfer to Sanur harbour. Fast boat to Nusa Penida (45 min). Check-in at beachfront resort. Afternoon relaxation and orientation walk. Sunset dinner at a cliffside restaurant.

Day 2 — West Nusa Penida Tour

Full-day west tour: Kelingking Beach viewpoint (optional beach descent), Angel's Billabong natural pool, Broken Beach natural arch, and Crystal Bay beach. Packed lunch included. Sunset swim at Crystal Bay.

Day 3 — Manta Ray & Snorkelling

Morning boat trip to Manta Point for snorkelling with oceanic manta rays. Afternoon snorkelling at Gamat Bay with sea turtles. Visit Atuh Beach — one of Nusa's hidden gems on the east coast.

Day 4 — East Nusa Penida & Teletubbies Hills

Morning: Explore the rolling green hills of Teletubbies Hill and Diamond Beach with its dramatic cliffside access. Afternoon: Peguyangan Waterfall and blue staircase. Evening: Traditional Nusa Penida dance performance.

Day 5 — Sunrise & Return to Bali

Early morning sunrise at Kelingking viewpoint for one final photograph. Breakfast and check-out. Fast boat back to Sanur. Transfer to Bali airport for departure.
