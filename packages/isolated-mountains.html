Package Overview

Discover the raw, untouched drama of Nusa Penida — Bali's most spectacular offshore island. This 5-day package takes you through sheer limestone cliffs, crystal-clear bays, and pristine white beaches that feel worlds away from the mainland. The iconic Kelingking Beach T-Rex cliffs and the manta ray snorkelling at Crystal Bay are experiences that define bucket-list travel.

Nusa Penida remains bracingly wild and authentic compared to mainland Bali. Roads are rough, the scenery is jaw-dropping, and the rewards for those who make the journey are extraordinary. Expert guides ensure you see the best of the island safely and comfortably.

Itinerary

Day 01 :
Arrival & Fast Boat to Nusa Penida

Arrive at Bali airport and transfer to Sanur harbour for a scenic 45-minute fast boat journey to Nusa Penida. Check in at your beachfront resort and spend the afternoon relaxing. End your day with a stunning sunset dinner at a cliffside restaurant overlooking the turquoise waters.

  • Arrival at Bali Airport
  • Transfer to Sanur Harbour
  • Fast Boat to Nusa Penida (45 min)
  • Check-in at Beachfront Resort
  • Afternoon Relaxation
  • Sunset Dinner at Cliffside Restaurant
Day 02 :
West Nusa Penida Tour

Embark on a full-day adventure exploring Western Nusa Penida's most iconic attractions. Marvel at the jaw-dropping Kelingking Beach viewpoint, swim in the natural infinity pool at Angel's Billabong, and witness the dramatic natural arch of Broken Beach. End with a relaxing sunset swim at Crystal Bay.

  • Kelingking Beach Viewpoint
  • Optional Beach Descent
  • Angel's Billabong Natural Pool
  • Broken Beach Natural Arch
  • Crystal Bay Beach
  • Packed Lunch Included
  • Sunset Swim
Day 03 :
Manta Ray & Snorkelling

Experience the thrill of swimming with majestic oceanic manta rays at Manta Point. Continue your underwater adventure with turtle snorkelling at Gamat Bay, then visit the hidden gem of Atuh Beach on the east coast with its dramatic rock formations.

  • Morning Boat Trip to Manta Point
  • Snorkelling with Manta Rays
  • Gamat Bay Turtle Snorkelling
  • Atuh Beach Visit
  • Beach Photography
Day 04 :
East Nusa Penida & Teletubbies Hills

Explore the rolling green hills of Teletubbies Hill and the dramatic Diamond Beach with its steep cliffside access. Visit the spiritual Peguyangan Waterfall with its iconic blue staircase. End your day with a traditional Nusa Penida dance performance.

  • Teletubbies Hills
  • Diamond Beach
  • Cliffside Access
  • Peguyangan Waterfall
  • Blue Staircase
  • Traditional Dance Performance
Day 05 :
Sunrise & Return to Bali

Wake up early for a spectacular sunrise at Kelingking viewpoint for one final unforgettable photograph. Enjoy breakfast, check out from your resort, and board the fast boat back to Sanur. Transfer to Bali airport for your departure with cherished memories.

  • Early Morning Sunrise at Kelingking
  • Final Photography Session
  • Breakfast
  • Check-out
  • Fast Boat to Sanur
  • Transfer to Bali Airport
