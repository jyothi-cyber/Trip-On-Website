Package Overview

Escape to the pristine paradise of Gili Trawangan — the largest of the three Gili Islands and one of Southeast Asia's most celebrated beach destinations. This 6-day package delivers the ultimate tropical island experience: powdery white sand, turquoise waters alive with sea turtles, world-class diving and snorkelling, and a wonderfully relaxed atmosphere with no motorised vehicles on the island.

Gili Trawangan effortlessly combines natural beauty with a vibrant social scene. By day you'll snorkel with hawksbill turtles metres from shore; by evening you'll watch the sun dip behind Bali's sacred Mount Agung from a beachside hammock while sipping fresh coconut. This is island life at its finest.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival & Island Welcome

Fly into Bali. Transfer to Padang Bai harbour. Fast boat to Gili Trawangan (2 hrs). Check-in at beachfront bungalow. Afternoon swim and beach relaxation. Welcome sunset cocktails and seafood dinner on the beach.

Day 2 — Snorkelling with Sea Turtles

Morning guided snorkelling tour at Turtle Heaven and Shark Point. Encounter wild hawksbill sea turtles in their natural habitat. Afternoon: Relax on the beach or explore the island by bicycle. Evening: Beachside BBQ dinner.

Day 3 — Scuba Diving Experience

Full-day diving session with certified PADI instructors at Gili's famous dive sites including Halik Reef and Shark Point. Beginners are welcome with a Discover Scuba course option. Afternoon free. Evening: Gili T's famous Swing Bar sunset gathering.

Day 4 — Gili Island Hopping

Take the local boat to explore Gili Air and Gili Meno — the quieter, more romantic neighbouring islands. Snorkel at Gili Meno's famous underwater sculpture garden. Picnic lunch on a private beach. Return to Trawangan by evening.

Day 5 — Cycling & Sunset Horse Ride

Morning: Cycle around the entire island (takes about 2 hours at a leisurely pace). Visit local pearl farms and artisan shops. Afternoon: Paddle boarding and kayaking. Evening: Horseback riding on the beach at sunset — a truly magical experience.

Day 6 — Last Swim & Departure

Final morning swim and breakfast on the beach. Check-out and fast boat back to Bali. Transfer to Ngurah Rai Airport. Depart with the island glow that only Gili Trawangan can give.
