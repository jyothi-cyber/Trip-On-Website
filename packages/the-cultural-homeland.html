Package Overview

Immerse yourself in the cultural soul of Bali with this 4-day journey through Ubud — Bali's artistic heartland. Nestled among emerald rice terraces and ancient forests, Ubud offers a profound encounter with traditional Balinese arts, sacred temples, and timeless rituals that have shaped this extraordinary island for centuries.

From the mystical Monkey Forest Sanctuary to the magnificent Ubud Palace where nightly dance performances light up the royal courtyard, every moment reveals a new layer of Balinese heritage. Expert local guides lead you through hidden temples, craft villages, and terraced landscapes.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival & Ubud Orientation

Arrive at Ngurah Rai Airport. Transfer to Ubud hotel. Evening stroll through Ubud Art Market. Welcome dinner at a valley-view warung. Kecak Fire Dance performance at Ubud Palace.

Day 2 — Temples, Forest & Rice Terraces

Visit Monkey Forest Sanctuary and Pura Dalem Agung temple. Explore Tegalalang Rice Terraces. Afternoon at Goa Gajah Elephant Cave. Evening traditional Balinese cooking class with dinner.

Day 3 — Art Villages & Coffee Plantation

Full-day art village tour: Celuk (silver), Mas (wood carving), Batuan (paintings). Luwak coffee plantation tasting. Yeh Pulu rock relief carvings. Evening Balinese spa treatment.

Day 4 — Sunrise & Departure

Optional: Early sunrise yoga session. Breakfast and free time for shopping at Ubud market. Check-out and transfer to airport with lasting memories.
