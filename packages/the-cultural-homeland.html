Package Overview

Immerse yourself in the cultural soul of Bali with this 4-day journey through Ubud — Bali's artistic heartland. Nestled among emerald rice terraces and ancient forests, Ubud offers a profound encounter with traditional Balinese arts, sacred temples, and timeless rituals that have shaped this extraordinary island for centuries.

From the mystical Monkey Forest Sanctuary to the magnificent Ubud Palace where nightly dance performances light up the royal courtyard, every moment reveals a new layer of Balinese heritage. Expert local guides lead you through hidden temples, craft villages, and terraced landscapes.

Itinerary

Day 01 :
Arrival In Bali Island Of The Gods!

Welcome to the Island of the Gods! After a long journey, you can finally begin your adventure and summon the magic of Bali. From stepping off the plane, you will experience the unlimited hospitality with which this beautiful island is imbibed. Embrace the next few days of your journey with unforgettable culture, adventure, and relaxation.

  • Arrival in Bali
  • Flower Garland Upon Arrival
  • Complimentary Mineral Water
  • Transfer to Hotel & Check In Hotel
  • Rest For the Day
Day 02 :
Ubud Exploration

Immerse yourself in the cultural heart of Bali. Visit the famous Monkey Forest Sanctuary, explore the stunning Tegalalang Rice Terraces, and discover ancient temples. Experience traditional Balinese culture firsthand with a cooking class in the evening.

  • Visit Monkey Forest Sanctuary
  • Pura Dalem Agung Temple
  • Tegalalang Rice Terraces
  • Goa Gajah Elephant Cave
  • Traditional Balinese Cooking Class
Day 03 :
Art Villages & Coffee Plantation

Discover Bali's artistic heritage with a full-day tour through traditional craft villages. Watch master silversmiths, wood carvers, and painters at work. Sample the famous Luwak coffee and relax with a traditional Balinese spa treatment.

  • Celuk Silver Village
  • Mas Wood Carving Village
  • Batuan Painting Village
  • Luwak Coffee Plantation Tasting
  • Balinese Spa Treatment
Day 04 :
Sunrise & Departure

Enjoy your final morning in paradise with an optional sunrise yoga session. Take time for last-minute shopping at the bustling Ubud market before checking out and transferring to the airport with unforgettable memories of your Bali adventure.

  • Transfer to Airport
