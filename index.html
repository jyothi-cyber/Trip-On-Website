Frequently asked questions

01

What is the best time of year to visit Bali ?

▾
The best time to visit Bali is during the dry season, from April to October. During these months, you can expect sunny weather, lower humidity, and minimal rainfall, making it perfect for beach activities, temple visits, and exploring the countryside.
02

What are the must-see attractions in Bali?

▴
Bali dazzles with its temples like Tanah Lot, the cultural hub of Ubud, the scenic Mount Batur, and stunning beaches such as Seminyak. Don't miss the tranquil Nusa Islands, the purifying Tirta Empul Temple, and the breathtaking Jatiluwih Rice Terraces for a comprehensive experience of Bali's rich culture and natural beauty.
03

Are there any visa requirements for travelers visiting Bali?

▾
Most tourists can obtain a Visa on Arrival (VoA) for 30 days at Indonesian airports and seaports. This visa can be extended once for an additional 30 days. Some nationalities may enter visa-free for up to 30 days.
04

Can the travel agency assist with booking accommodations and activities in Bali?

▾
Absolutely! Our travel agency provides end-to-end planning assistance including hotel bookings, activity reservations, airport transfers, and curated itineraries tailored to your preferences and budget.
05

What are the recommended vaccinations or health precautions for travelers to Bali?

▾
It's recommended to be up-to-date on routine vaccinations such as Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B, and Typhoid. Consult your doctor about malaria prevention if you plan to visit rural areas. Always carry mosquito repellent and drink bottled water.
06

What is the local currency in Bali, and are credit cards widely accepted?

▾
The local currency is the Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Credit cards are accepted in major hotels, restaurants, and shops in tourist areas. However, it's advisable to carry cash for smaller vendors, local markets, and rural areas.
