Everything you should know about Kuta, Gali

Trip On Editorial
Updated July 2026

Kuta and the Gili Islands form one of Bali's most iconic coastal experiences. Kuta's long golden beach has legendary surf breaks that attract riders from around the world, while the three Gili islands — Trawangan, Air, and Meno — offer turquoise lagoons, coral reefs teeming with sea turtles, and a laid-back island lifestyle free from motorised vehicles.

History:

Kuta was a small fishing village and the first port of contact for European and American traders who arrived in Bali during the 19th century. The Dutch colonial presence established a trading post here, and the beach gradually transformed from a commercial landing point into a popular retreat.

The 1960s and 1970s saw Kuta emerge as a global surfing destination when pioneering surfers discovered its consistent right-hand beach breaks. Cheap guesthouses, surf board rentals, and beachside warungs made it a backpacker haven, and its reputation spread rapidly across the surfing world.

The Gili Islands, located off the northwest coast of Lombok, remained virtually unknown until the 1980s when early eco-travellers began arriving by wooden boat. Their no-motor-vehicle policy, preserved coral gardens, and relaxed pace quickly made them one of Southeast Asia's most beloved island escapes.

Beach Attractions:

Kuta Beach stretches for nearly three kilometres, backed by a bustling strip of surf shops, restaurants, and nightlife venues. At dusk, hundreds of visitors gather on the sand to watch Bali's most spectacular sunsets painting the sky in vivid orange and pink.

Pura Segara, a small sea temple at the southern end of Kuta Beach, is where local priests perform daily offerings to appease the spirits of the ocean. Visitors are welcome to observe the ceremonies from a respectful distance, gaining a rare glimpse of living Balinese Hindu tradition.

On Gili Trawangan, the famous "swing over the sea" has become an iconic landmark. The island's main strip hosts some of the region's best dive operators, and snorkelling directly off the beach reveals hawksbill sea turtles grazing on the shallow coral just metres from shore.
