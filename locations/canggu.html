Everything you should know about Canggu

Trip On Editorial
Updated July 2026

Canggu is Bali's most talked-about neighbourhood — a creative melting pot where rice paddies meet surf breaks and digital nomads share co-working spaces with visiting surfers and yoga retreaters. Once a sleepy coastal village north of Seminyak, Canggu's Instagram-friendly cafes, black-sand beaches, and vibrant social scene have made it the unofficial capital of modern Bali lifestyle.

History:

Canggu's history stretches back centuries as a traditional Balinese farming and fishing community. The area's volcanic black-sand beaches and reliable surf breaks were known to local fishermen long before tourists arrived, and the paddy fields that still frame the village today have been cultivated for generations.

The early surfing community discovered Canggu's Echo Beach and Batu Bolong breaks in the 1990s, establishing small guesthouses and surf camps that attracted a trickle of adventurous travellers. The neighbourhood retained its quiet village character well into the 2000s.

The digital nomad revolution of the 2010s transformed Canggu more dramatically than any other Bali neighbourhood. High-speed internet, affordable co-working spaces, and a sociable international community made it a magnet for location-independent workers, triggering an explosion of cafes, boutique gyms, and trendy accommodation.

Iconic Spots:

Batu Bolong Temple is Canggu's spiritual centrepiece — a small Hindu sea temple perched dramatically on a black rock at the water's edge, framed by crashing waves and sunset light. Daily ceremonies fill the air with incense and gamelan music, offering a grounding counterpoint to the neighbourhood's trendy cafe culture.

Echo Beach is Canggu's most celebrated surf spot, offering punchy left and right-hand reef breaks that attract intermediate and advanced surfers. The beachfront is lined with warung restaurants and surf schools, and watching skilled surfers from a clifftop cafe at sunset has become a Canggu institution.

Canggu's cafe scene is legendary — venues like Deus Ex Machina, The Shady Shack, and Finn's Beach Club set the template for the hybrid bar-restaurant-social-space concept. Rice paddy cafes offer cold brew coffee and avocado toast against views of jade-green terraces.
