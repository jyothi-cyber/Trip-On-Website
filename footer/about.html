Who We Are

Born from a Love for Bali

Trip On was founded by a group of passionate travelers who fell in love with Bali and decided to share its magic with the world. We are not just a travel agency — we are your friends in Bali, ready to craft experiences that stay with you forever.

With over 5 years of experience and 100+ happy travelers, we have perfected the art of Bali travel. Every trip we plan is personal, thoughtful, and built around what matters most to you.

  • 100% customized itineraries for every traveler
  • Expert local guides with deep cultural knowledge
  • 24/7 support throughout your journey
  • Best-price guarantee on all packages
Our Mission

Creating Memories That Last a Lifetime

Our mission is simple: to make your Bali dream a reality. We believe travel should be effortless, enriching, and deeply personal. Whether you are honeymooning in Seminyak, trekking Mount Batur at sunrise, or exploring the temples of Ubud, we make sure every moment counts.

We work with a handpicked network of local hotels, guides, and experience providers who share our commitment to excellence and authenticity.

  • Authentic Balinese cultural experiences
  • Sustainable and responsible tourism practices
  • Partnerships with top-rated local vendors
  • Transparent pricing — no hidden costs
